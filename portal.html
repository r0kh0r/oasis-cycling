FAQRace rules

Frequently asked questions.

How do I enter the draw? ENTRY

Create an account with your email. That alone earns your first ticket. Predicting podiums earns more, up to 16 in total. No purchase is ever necessary to enter or win.

How do the predictions work? PREDICTIONS

Pick the full podium, 1st through 3rd, for each race before the deadline. Every place you get exactly right earns one ticket, up to three per race and 16 across the season. We email you each result, win or lose.

What can I win? PRIZES

A Canyon Endurance CF 5 race bike for first, a signed Slovenia team jersey for second, three team jerseys for places three through five, and five Canyon helmets for places six through ten. Ten winners in total, drawn from all tickets when the season ends.

Do I need crypto to participate? REQUIREMENTS

No. Entering and predicting is completely free. Your account is a secure wallet created from your email through Turnkey: no seed phrase, nothing to buy.

What's the campaign portal vs Privana? ARCHITECTURE

The portal is where you predict and track your tickets. Privana is the product behind it, a non-custodial private trading app built on Oasis. Both share the same Turnkey wallet, so when the portal retires your account simply continues as a Privana account. No migration, no new login.

When are winners drawn? THE DRAW

At the close of the season, once the final race is decided. Winners are drawn at random from all tickets: everyone with a ticket has a chance, and more tickets mean better odds. Winners are notified by email.
